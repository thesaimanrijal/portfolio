I built this site from scratch, which was not the efficient choice. There are
templates that would have had something online in an afternoon, and they would
have looked better than this does.

But I wanted to understand every piece of it. There is a difference between
having a website and knowing how your website works, and that difference tends
to show up at the worst possible moment — usually when something breaks and you
discover you have no idea which layer to look at.

So this is the slow version. A handful of pages, some markdown files, and a
build step I can explain.

## What goes here

Mostly notes on things I am working through rather than finished conclusions.
Some of it will be about the mathematics underneath machine learning, which is
the part I keep returning to. Some of it will be about whatever else has my
attention.

Writing things down forces a kind of honesty that thinking about them doesn't.
You find out fairly quickly whether you actually had a thought or just a feeling
about one.
